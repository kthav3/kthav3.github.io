What makes a brand memorable?

Some brands are recognizable before you even see their name. A color, a photograph or the design of a store can tell you who they are. This site explores the choices behind those impressions and how marketing becomes part of everyday life.

A brand's identity includes its name, logo, colors, language and overall appearance. This section explores how those elements work together to communicate a personality and appeal to an audience.

Questions to investigate: What makes a brand recognizable? How can its appearance influence what people expect?
